# EmptyState

Empty states fill a page or panel that has nothing yet, explain what will be there, and offer the first step.

## The consumer provides

- `icon` (the area's icon), `title` (what this place is for, as an invitation), `children` (one or two sentences), `actions` (one primary, one secondary at most).

## Rules

- The icon tile carries the folio corner in `gold`; no illustrations of people.
- Distinguish "nothing yet" (invite) from "no results" (offer to clear filters) from "error" (say what failed and retry).
