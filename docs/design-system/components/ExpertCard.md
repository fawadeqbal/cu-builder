# ExpertCard

Expert cards introduce a vetted expert in the marketplace: who they are, their rating, what they offer and the starting price.

## The consumer provides

- `name`, `headline`, `rating`, `reviews`, `services`, `languages`, `priceFrom`.

## Rules

- Real photos when the expert uploads one, initials otherwise. No stock imagery.
- The rating always shows its count. New experts with no reviews say "New" in a `highlight` badge instead of stars.
- "Book" opens the booking dialog; the card itself opens the profile.
