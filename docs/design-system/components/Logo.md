# Logo

The logo is the folio mark (a blue page with a gold folded corner and two text lines) beside the wordmark "CV Builder" in Schibsted Grotesk 750.

## The consumer provides

- `size` (28 in the app bar; 20 minimum), `wordmark` (false for the mark alone: favicons, tight spaces), `href`, `product` (white-label partners).

## Rules

- Never recolour, outline, rotate or add effects. In dark theme the page turns light blue and the text lines dark (it reads its colours from tokens).
- Clear space around it equals the fold's width (a quarter of the mark).
- White-label partners replace the whole logo with theirs; "Made with CV Builder" moves to the footer unless hidden.
- Files for other uses (app icon, favicon, dark and light marks) are in Assets › Logos.
