Save this job

Connect the extension to your CV Builder account to save jobs and tailor your CV.

  1. Sign in to CV Builder and open Account → Browser extension.
  2. Create a key and copy it (it starts with cvb_).
  3. Paste it in the extension's settings.

You only do this once.

Description

Saves the job, then opens CV Builder to review AI suggestions in a new copy of your CV. Your original isn't changed.
